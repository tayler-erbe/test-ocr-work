03 / METHODS & REPRODUCIBILITY

Evidence first.
From the page to the decision.

I brought desktop and laptop experiments into a shared evaluation contract, then tested a source-linked extraction approach. The scores come from reference answers and code—not another model’s opinion.

  1. 01

    Preserve the source

    Track document hashes, page identity and explicit unread-page states.

  2. 02

    Parse or read images

    Native text where available; local OCR and vision for scans, tables and charts.

  3. 03

    Select evidence

    Retrieve candidate spans; ask the model to select evidence IDs.

  4. 04

    Validate and review

    Copy source text in code, validate provenance, and retain human review.

What makes the comparison inspectable?

Frozen cases, prompts, schemas and scoring rules. Recorded model digests and runtime settings. Failed and truncated cases remain in the evaluation. Separate development choices from confirmation tests.

Public data includes ChartQA, OmniDocBench, CUAD and ContractNLI. Each answers a different question; none alone certifies institutional accuracy.

Read the corrected routing report ↗
Metric definitions

CER: character edits relative to the target; lower is better. The explorer reports the source’s completed-CER metric and shows failure counts separately. TEDS: table tree similarity, not a guarantee of correct amounts. Relaxed chart accuracy: reference matching with numeric tolerance. Value recall: recovered chart values. Word F1: word overlap, not semantic correctness or reading-order fidelity.

Confirmation, uncertainty and leakage

Round 2 froze 586 development and 1,322 confirmation questions across a chart-disjoint split; the confirmation questions cover 722 charts. Multiple questions per chart are correlated. Reported intervals and Holm corrections are preserved; do not interpret question-level uncertainty as independently sampled charts. “Fresh” means unused in earlier project runs, not proven absent from pretraining.

Vote3 was the selected confirmatory chart method. Self-check remains a supporting comparison. Small 50–60-case tasks are exploratory.

Can the machines reproduce each other?

Qwen2.5-VL 7B used matching weights: 11 of 13 groups reproduced under the report’s comparison rule, with two disagreements. The laptop’s 8B digest differs from the desktop record, so it is a method comparison, not a same-weight reproduction. A 7B/8B run does not reproduce a 32B result.

What has not been established?

Production readiness, real-document semantic accuracy, a universal best model, sustained L4/L40S throughput, or broad numeric fidelity on multi-page budgets. Source-traceability checks cannot prove an answer is relevant or correct.

04 / THE HARDWARE CONSTRAINT

Fit the workload
to the machine.

The deployment targets are a single L4 (24 GB) or a single L40S (48 GB). Fit comes from measured weight sizes, with room left for context, images and concurrency. Speed was measured on one desktop, so it compares models fairly but runs faster than either target card.

05 / BEYOND OCR: EVIDENCE-LINKED EXTRACTION

Exact source text is a check.
Correct meaning is another.

Legal experiments exposed quote-to-source binding failures. The evidence-by-ID design lets the model select spans while code copies and verifies the text.

Models propose evidence selections; application code retrieves the source text and checks its location. This separates mechanical traceability from semantic correctness. Public legal benchmarks are used to investigate both. No private-document results are included in this public case study.

06 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Make document-bound
data usable.

An ERP migration can move the records without unlocking the information buried in their attachments.

A single packet can combine a research proposal, an award notice, scanned signatures, a spreadsheet exported to PDF, budget tables and charts. The information is readable to a person but difficult to search, reconcile or load into structured systems. This is the operational problem the project targets; its prevalence and financial impact were not measured in this evaluation.

The intended workflow turns these packets into reviewable records: identify document types, recover text and tables, propose defined fields, attach page-level evidence, flag missing or uncertain information, and export approved data for downstream systems.

Research proposals
and awards

Candidate fields include sponsor, project identifier, investigator, performance period, award amount, budget categories, milestones and reporting obligations.

Procurement
and supplier packets

Candidate fields include supplier, solicitation identifier, line items, prices, payment terms, delivery commitments and supporting compliance documents.

Other document-heavy
workflows

Contracts and amendments; invoices and purchase orders; financial reports; technical reports; administrative forms; archival scans. These are potential applications, not individually validated deployments.

Control the data.
Measure the trade-offs.

Where policy or contractual restrictions prevent external processing, a local pipeline can keep document content within an organization-controlled environment. That is an architectural option, not a security certification: access controls, encryption, logging, retention, network egress and software supply-chain controls still require implementation and verification. NIST AI 600-1 provides the risk-management context.

The deployment targets are a single NVIDIA L4 or L40S. Every recommended model fits an L40S; on an L4 the parsers and a 20B-class legal model fit, while a 32B model is tight. On public data, the confirmed table route, PaddleOCR-VL-1.6, reached 0.852 TEDS and 97.4% numeric recall on 100 financial-report tables. These results support a review-assisted prototype. They do not establish reliable unattended extraction from research awards or procurement packets.

Budget estimates are dated, not measured savings. A small-parser workstation has a planning allowance of $1,500–$2,500; an L4-compatible server $6,000–$10,000; an L40S-compatible server $16,000–$21,000. These combine dated card listings with explicit host allowances; see the hardware guide for stock, warranty and exclusions. These alternative machines were not benchmarked. Hardware purchase price alone also excludes engineering, review, maintenance and energy costs. No production total-cost or return-on-investment claim is made.

Original spreadsheets should be parsed directly when available; a spreadsheet rendered into a PDF requires a different recovery path. Multi-page packet segmentation, chart interpretation and field-level validation each need their own quality gate.

My contribution: evaluation orchestration, protocol reconciliation, local integration testing, failure analysis, evidence validation and the reporting interface. AI coding assistants supported implementation; local models performed benchmark reading and extraction. The public case study uses public benchmark evidence.
